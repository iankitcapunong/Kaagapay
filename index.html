Questions

Things people ask before applying.

Can't find it here? Read all FAQs or message our team.

How fast will I get the money?

Most complete applications get a decision the same business day. Once you sign the Disclosure Statement, the money is sent right away. E-wallet transfers usually arrive within minutes, and InstaPay bank transfers within the hour.

Will this affect my credit record?

We report to the Credit Information Corporation (CIC), as lenders in the Philippines are required to. Paying on time builds your record. Late payments can hurt it.

What happens if I pay late?

A late payment charge applies from the day after your due date. The exact amount is in your Disclosure Statement before you sign. If you tell us before the due date, we'll work out a new schedule with you.

Can I pay off my loan early?

Yes, at any time. You only pay interest up to the day you settle, and there's no pre-termination charge.

Why was my application declined?

Usually because of a blurry ID photo, a name mismatch between your ID and payout account, or income we couldn't verify. You can reapply after 30 days.
