Applying

Who can apply?

Filipino citizens living in the Philippines, aged 21 to 65, with a regular source of income and a mobile number registered in their own name.

What documents do I need?

One valid government ID and a selfie. We accept PhilSys National ID, UMID, Driver's License, Passport, SSS, PRC, Postal and Voter's ID. You don't need a payslip, co-maker or collateral.

How long does approval take?

Most complete applications get a decision the same business day. Applications sent late at night or on Sundays are reviewed the next morning.

Why was my application declined?

The common reasons are a blurry or cropped ID photo, a name that doesn't match your payout account, or income we couldn't verify. You can reapply after 30 days.

Can I have more than one loan at a time?

No. You can apply for a new loan once your current one is fully paid.

Costs and interest

How is interest calculated?

Interest is 0.2% per day on the amount you borrowed, for the number of days you have the loan. On ₱5,000 for 90 days that's ₱900, so you repay ₱5,900 in total.

Are there other fees?

There's no processing, service or approval fee. A late payment charge only applies if you pay after your due date, and the amount is written in your Disclosure Statement before you sign.

What is a Disclosure Statement?

A document required by the Truth in Lending Act (R.A. 3765). It lists the amount you'll receive, the interest, every charge, the total you'll repay and your payment schedule. You review and sign it before any money is released.

Is the amount I receive the same as what I borrow?

Yes. We don't deduct anything from your loan before sending it.

Repaying

How do I pay?

Through GCash, Maya, online banking or over the counter at partners like 7-Eleven and Bayad Center. See the step-by-step guide.

Can I pay early?

Yes. Interest stops on the day you pay in full, and there's no pre-termination charge.

What if I can't pay on my due date?

Message us before the due date. We can usually move it or split the balance into smaller payments. Any change is confirmed in writing with the new total.

Does paying on time help me?

Yes. It raises your limit for your next loan and builds your record with the Credit Information Corporation (CIC).

Privacy and safety

Will you contact my family or friends?

No. We don't access your phone contacts, and we never contact anyone about your loan except you. Debt-shaming is prohibited under SEC Memorandum Circular No. 18, s. 2019, and we don't do it.

What do you do with my data?

We use it to check your identity, assess your application and service your loan, in line with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (R.A. 10173). Details are in our Privacy Policy.

How do I know this is really you?

Check our name against the SEC list of recorded online lending platforms. We only message from our official number, and we'll never ask for a fee before releasing your loan.
