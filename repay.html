How to pay your loan

Choose a channel below. Whichever you use, you'll need your loan reference number. It's on your account dashboard and in every SMS reminder we send.

  1. Open GCash or Maya and go to BillsLook for the "Loans" or "Lending" category.
  2. Search for "Kaagapay"Choose us from the list of billers. Don't send money to a personal number.
  3. Enter your loan reference numberIt starts with KGP and is 10 characters long, for example KGP4K82Q1Z.
  4. Enter the amount and confirmPay the full amount due, or at least the minimum shown in your account.
  5. Keep the receiptYour payment shows in your account within 15 minutes. We'll also send an SMS.

Your options

Full payment, early payment, or a little more time.

Pay early, pay less

Settle any day before your due date and interest stops that day. No pre-termination charge. Your account shows the exact payoff amount for today.

Need more time?

Message us before your due date. We can usually move your due date or split what's left into smaller payments. Any change is confirmed in writing, with the new total, before it takes effect.

Only pay through the channels on this page. Our staff will never ask you to send a payment to a personal GCash number or bank account. If someone does, don't pay, and report it to us.
