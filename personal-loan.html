Personal cash loan

A loan for bigger plans, with no collateral. Borrow ₱100,000 to ₱2,000,000 over 6–60 months, sent to your bank or e-wallet. Every peso of cost is shown before you apply.

Apply for this loan Use the calculator
What to prepare

4 documents

  1. One valid government IDPhilSys, UMID, Driver's License, Passport, SSS, PRC, Postal or Voter's ID.
  2. A selfieTaken on your phone during the application, so we know it's really you.
  3. Proof of incomeLatest 3 payslips or COE, ITR, or business permit with 3 to 6 months of bank statements.
  4. Proof of billingA recent electricity, water or internet bill at your current address.

The amount we approve depends on your income and how much you already pay on other loans. Your bank or e-wallet account must be in your own name.

Good to know

  • Amount₱100K – ₱2M
  • Terms6–60 months
  • Interest1.5% per month
  • Fees₱0
  • CollateralNone

Questions

About the personal cash loan

  • No collateral and no co-maker
  • Money sent to your bank or e-wallet
  • No fees before your loan is released
How fast will I get the money?

Once your documents are complete, most decisions go out within 1 to 3 business days. After you sign, the money is sent to your account.

Can I pay off my loan early?

Yes, any time, with no pre-termination charge. You won't pay interest for the months you don't use.

How much can I borrow?

From ₱100,000 to ₱2,000,000. The exact amount depends on your income and your other monthly loan payments.

Will this affect my credit record?

We report to the Credit Information Corporation (CIC), as lenders are required to. Paying on time builds your record.
