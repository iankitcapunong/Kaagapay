Personal cash loan

A small loan for everyday needs, with no collateral. Borrow ₱2,000 to ₱25,000 and get it in GCash, Maya or your bank. Every peso of cost is shown before you apply.

What to prepare

2 documents

  1. One valid government IDPhilSys, UMID, Driver's License, Passport, SSS, PRC, Postal or Voter's ID.
  2. A selfieTaken on your phone during the application, so we know it's really you.

Optional: a payslip, COE or recent e-wallet history can raise the amount we approve. Your GCash, Maya or bank account must be in your own name.

Questions

About the personal cash loan

  • No collateral, no co-maker, no payslip required
  • Money sent to GCash, Maya or your bank
  • No fees before your loan is released
How fast will I get the money?

Most complete applications get a decision the same business day. Once you sign, e-wallet transfers usually arrive within minutes.

Can I pay off my loan early?

Yes, any time. Interest stops on the day you pay in full, and there's no pre-termination charge.

How do I get a higher limit?

Repay your first loan on time. Your limit goes up on your next loan, up to ₱25,000.

Will this affect my credit record?

We report to the Credit Information Corporation (CIC), as lenders are required to. Paying on time builds your record.
