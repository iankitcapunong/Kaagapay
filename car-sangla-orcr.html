Car Sangla ORCR

Borrow against your car's OR/CR and keep driving it while you pay. We hold the documents and annotate a chattel mortgage on the CR until your loan is fully paid.

Apply for this loan Ask on Messenger
What to prepare

5 documents

  1. Original OR/CRRegistered in your name. If the car isn't transferred to you yet, tell us and send the deed of sale.
  2. Two valid government IDsPhilSys, UMID, Driver's License, Passport, SSS, PRC or Postal ID.
  3. Proof of billingA recent electricity, water or internet bill at your current address.
  4. Proof of incomeLatest payslip or COE, ITR, or business permit with recent bank or e-wallet statements.
  5. Photos of the carFront, back, both sides, odometer, and the chassis and engine numbers.

Clear phone photos are fine for the application. We'll tell you if we need to see an original.

Questions

About Car Sangla ORCR

  • No fees before your loan is released
  • You keep driving your car the whole time
  • One person follows your file from start to release
Can I still use my car?

Yes. Only the OR/CR is held. The car stays with you and you drive it as usual.

What if the OR/CR isn't in my name yet?

Tell us when you apply. In some cases a notarized deed of sale is accepted. Otherwise the car needs to be transferred to your name first.

How much can I borrow?

It depends on the appraised value of your car, mainly its year, model, mileage and condition. You'll see the exact amount before you sign anything.

What happens when I finish paying?

We return your OR/CR and release the chattel mortgage annotated on the CR.

Ready to apply with your car?

Tell us about your car. We'll tell you exactly what to prepare next.
