Real Estate (Sangla Titulo)

Unlock the value of your titled property for bigger plans: a business, renovation, tuition or debt consolidation. Amounts are larger and terms longer, but the title check takes more time than other loans.

What to prepare

6 documents

  1. Copy of the land titleTCT or CCT in your name. If it's in a parent's or co-owner's name, they'll need to join the application.
  2. Latest tax declarationFor the land, and for the building if there's a house on it.
  3. Real property tax receiptYour latest amilyar payment, or a tax clearance from the municipal or city treasurer.
  4. Two valid government IDsPhilSys, UMID, Driver's License, Passport, SSS, PRC or Postal ID.
  5. Proof of billingA recent electricity, water or internet bill at your current address.
  6. Photos of the propertyFront, sides and street view, plus a pin or vicinity map of the location.

Start with clear phone photos. We'll ask for certified copies only once your application moves forward.

Questions

About Sangla Titulo

  • No fees before your loan is released
  • You keep living in or using the property
  • One person follows your file from start to release
Why does this loan take longer?

We verify the title with the Registry of Deeds, appraise the property, and register the mortgage. Each step protects both you and us, and some depend on government offices.

The title is in my parents' name. Can I still apply?

Yes, but the registered owners have to join the loan as co-borrowers or sign a Special Power of Attorney. Tell us when you apply so we can guide you.

I'm married. Does my spouse need to sign?

Usually yes. For property owned by a married couple, the spouse's written consent is generally needed to mortgage it.

What happens when I finish paying?

We give you a release of mortgage so the annotation can be cancelled on your title.
